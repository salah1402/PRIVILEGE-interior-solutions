2. **Template Remnants in Project Cards**:

   - The 5 Home gallery cards contain commented-out title tags: `<!-- <h3>Explore the world</h3> -->`.

4. **All Projects Labeled "Residential" (`cat-1`)**:
   - While the project navigation shows filters for "All", "Residential", "Ecommercial", "Office", and "Hospital", every single one of the 52 project entries has class `.cat-1`. The other categories are completely empty.

5. **Local Asset Scarcity**:
   - The only high-quality local client imagery preserved on disk is `logo-new.png` and `about-banner.jpg`. The 52 project images and 5 gallery images are linked to the external server domain.

6. **Grammar & Copy Idiosyncrasies in Original Text**:
   - "Since 2001, we have been providing great interior solutions and customer service for homes, Shops, Social and commercial clients..." (random capitalization of Shops and Social).
   - "We provide a architectural 3D modeling services." (incorrect article "a").
